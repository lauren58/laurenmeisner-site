The TikTok experiment

We told people to watch
TikTok like a podcast.
It worked.

In May 2026 we launched a long-form TikTok account for Mini Scroll. We were watching user behaviour shift and audiences become increasingly platform-agnostic, so we tested a theory: young people would watch an entire podcast on TikTok, and if they liked it, they would keep coming back. We publish Mini Scroll’s full and unedited stories — some are up to 20+ minutes long — with an on-screen prompt telling viewers to listen to it like a podcast. In under five months we built a cult following that watches Mini Scroll on TikTok specifically, and partners like Colgate, ING and Jurlique have jumped on board.

The founder

Lauren Meisner

Lauren Meisner is a new media founder, internet culture strategist, and leading voice on the attention economy based in Sydney.

She is the Founder & Director of Centennial World, an award-winning Gen Z media network she bootstrapped to 20 million reach per month, and the founder of SponStudio, a platform rethinking how podcasts get funded.

In 2019 she spotted a gap for a youth publisher covering internet culture and the creator economy with the seriousness those subjects deserved, and built Centennial World to fill it. She began her career as a writer in digital publishing, with bylines in ELLE Australia, Harper's BAZAAR Australia and more.
